good pitching stats (defense)
    innings pitched
    ERA - earned run average per 9 innings pitched
    FIP - basically ERA but removes every outcome that isn't a hr,walk,hit by pitch,strikeouts
    ERA+ - era but adjust for certain factors so you can compare different eras of baseball - this will put all pitchers on a even playing field
    WHIP - walks hits allowed per inning pitchs - if a pitchers whip is 1.5 every 2 innings a base runner is going to get on base
    the /9s
        bb/9 - walks - lower the better
        k/9 - strikeouts - higher the better
        hr/9 - home runs allowed - lower the better

good hitting stats (offense)
    BA - batting average - 4 out 10 abs = .400 BA - doesnt factor walks or getting base-hits like doubles triples and homers
    OBP - on base percentage - same as ba but gives you credit for walking - which a walk is the same amoint of bases as a single
    OPS - on base plus slugging - adds together on b ase percentage and slugging percentage - how often you are getting on base and driving for power - leaders are always the leaders in the league
    OPS+ - this like era+ makes the league average 100. so if the stat reads 160 it is 60% better than all the players in the league
    SB - stolen bases - this is only for fast players
    HR - homeruns -
